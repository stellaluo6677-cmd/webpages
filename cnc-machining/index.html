What We Do

Machining
Capabilities

Integrated CNC machining from prototype to production — combining advanced equipment with experienced engineering support for complex geometries and demanding tolerances.

01
Multi-Axis CNC Milling
3, 4 and 5-axis milling for intricate parts, tight profiles, and close-tolerance features. Ideal for complex housings, brackets, and structural components requiring multi-face machining in a single setup.
02
CNC Turning
High-precision turning for small to medium-sized cylindrical parts. Supports live tooling for combined milling and turning operations, reducing setups and improving dimensional consistency.
03
Wire & EDM
Precision cutting of hard metals and fine features with minimal distortion. Essential for tool steels, carbide inserts, and parts requiring sharp internal corners or ultra-tight tolerances.
04
Surface & Form Grinding
Achieves superior flatness, parallelism, and fine surface finish for critical mating faces. Used for hardened tool components, precision guides, and parts requiring Ra < 0.4 µm finishes.
05
Threading, Tapping & Assembly
In-house threading, tapping, and mechanical assembly operations. Eliminates outsourcing delays — from raw material to fully assembled sub-components, delivered under one roof.
06
Surface Treatments
Complete finishing options including anodizing, plating, and custom coatings. Full in-house inspection with CMMs, projectors, and optical comparators ensures every part ships to spec.
