Methodology

Our DFM Review Process

A structured, analytical data cycle designed to transition components seamlessly from a virtual CAD draft into flawless physical reality.

Phase 01 / Input

Model Data Intake

We begin with a thorough technical extraction of your provided 2D blueprints and detailed 3D geometric models.

Phase 02 / Analysis

Predictive Core Diagnostics

Our engineers analyze geometry, tolerances, and materials. We isolate deformation risks in stamping, warpage or shrinkage fields in molding, and machining path limitations for highly complex structures.

Phase 03 / Optimization

Actionable Execution

We deliver clear feedback featuring optimized dimensions and simplified structures. Small adjustments drastically lower tooling complexity and shorten development cycles.
